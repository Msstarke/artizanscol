FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about how Artizans Collective works — for artists and clients.

For clients

Hiring artists

How do I find the right artist?

Use the Explore page to filter by category, medium, availability, and budget. Each profile shows the artist's starting price, a brief about section, and portfolio samples. Browse until you find someone whose work and budget fit your brief, then send a booking request directly from their profile.

How does the booking request work?

From any artist's profile page, fill in the booking form with your project type, description, timeline, and budget. The artist receives your request and can accept or decline. If they accept, you'll be connected directly to discuss details. There's no auction, no bidding, and no platform middleman in the conversation.

Are these profiles real human artists?

Yes. Every profile on the platform is reviewed by our team before going live. We check that the portfolio represents genuine human-made work — not AI-generated content passed off as original. If a profile is found to be in breach of this, it is removed. Verification is the entire point of the platform.

Do I need an account to browse artists?

No — the Explore page and artist profiles are fully public. You only need an account to send a booking request. Creating an account is free and takes less than a minute.

What does the starting price mean?

The starting price shown on a profile is the artist's minimum engagement rate for new projects. Final pricing depends on scope, timeline, and deliverables — the booking request is where you describe your brief and the artist confirms fit. Think of starting price as a floor, not a quote.

For artists

Publishing your profile

How do I get my profile live?

Create an account, opt in to the artist side, then complete three required fields: your primary category, at least one medium, and a starting price. Once those are set, your profile moves from draft to ready-to-publish. Toggle your profile to visible from the workspace and it goes live immediately for clients to discover.

What counts as human-made work for verification?

Work that you made — using traditional or digital tools — without generative AI producing the final output. Referencing AI for research, colour palette suggestions, or brief ideation does not disqualify work. Using AI to generate images, copy, or other deliverables that you then present as your own does. If you're unsure whether your practice qualifies, contact us before submitting.

Is there a cost to list on the platform?

Creating an account and publishing a profile is free. There is no listing fee and no commission taken from direct bookings made through the platform. We plan to introduce optional paid features in future — these will always be opt-in and clearly labelled.

Can I take my profile down at any time?

Yes. You can toggle your profile visibility off at any time from your workspace. This immediately removes it from public discovery and the Explore page. Your data is retained so you can republish when ready. If you want your account and data deleted entirely, contact us at support@artizanscollective.com.

Will beginners get visibility alongside established artists?

Yes. Discovery on Artizans Collective is not ranked by follower count, popularity score, or platform engagement metrics. Clients browse by category, medium, and budget fit. A strong, honest profile with clear work samples competes on the same terms as a well-known name. We're not trying to replicate the dynamics that already disadvantage emerging creators.

Platform

How it all works

What categories does the platform support?

Currently: illustration, photography, graphic design, motion and animation, music and audio, writing and copy, and fine arts. We plan to expand this as the platform grows. If your practice doesn't fit neatly into an existing category, choose the closest one and describe your work clearly in your profile bio.

How do disputes or problems get resolved?

If you have a problem with a booking, a profile, or another user, contact us at support@artizanscollective.com. We review disputes case by case. For verification concerns specifically — if you believe a profile contains AI-generated work — use the trust contact at trust@artizanscollective.com.

Is my data private?

Yes. We collect only what's needed to run the platform — account details, profile content, and booking history. We do not sell data to third parties. See the Legal page for the full privacy policy.
