for clients

how do i find the right artist?

head to explore and filter by category, medium, availability and budget. every profile shows the artist's starting price, a short about section and portfolio samples. browse until someone fits your brief, then send a booking request straight from their profile.

how does a booking request work?

on any artist's profile, fill in the booking form with your project type, description, timeline and budget. the artist gets your request and can accept or decline. if they accept, you're connected directly to sort out details. no auction, no bidding, no middleman.

are these real human artists?

yes. every profile is checked by our team before it goes live. we make sure the portfolio is genuine human-made work, not ai content passed off as original. if a profile breaks that, it's removed. verification is the whole point of this place.

do i need an account to browse?

no — explore and the artist profiles are fully public. you only need an account to send a booking request. making one is free and takes under a minute.

what does the starting price mean?

it's the artist's minimum rate. the final price depends on scope, timeline and deliverables — that's what the booking form is for. think of the starting price as a floor, not a fixed quote.

for artists

how do i get my profile live?

make an account, opt in to the artist side, then fill in three things: your main category, at least one medium and a starting price. once that's set, your profile goes from draft to ready. flip it visible in your workspace and it's live straight away.

what counts as human-made work?

work you made with traditional or digital tools, without generative ai producing the final result. using ai for research or ideas is fine. using ai to generate images, copy or audio that you then pass off as your own isn't. not sure if your work qualifies? message us before you submit.

does it cost anything to list?

making an account and publishing a profile is free. no listing fee, no cut taken from direct bookings. we might add optional paid features later — those will always be opt-in and clearly labelled.

can i take my profile down whenever?

yes. flip your profile visibility off in your workspace any time and it's pulled from public discovery straight away. your data stays so you can republish when you're ready. for full account and data deletion, email support@artizanscollective.com.

do beginners get seen next to big names?

yes. discovery here isn't ranked by follower count or engagement. clients browse by category, medium and budget. a strong honest profile with clear samples competes on the same terms as a well-known name.

platform

what categories do you support?

right now: illustration, photography, graphic design, motion and animation, music and audio, writing and copy, and fine arts. if your work doesn't fit neatly into one, pick the closest and explain it in your profile bio.

how are disputes sorted out?

email support@artizanscollective.com. we look at disputes case by case. if you think a profile contains ai-generated work, flag it at trust@artizanscollective.com.

is my data private?

yes. we only collect what we need to run the platform, and we don't sell your data to anyone. the full privacy policy is on the legal page.
