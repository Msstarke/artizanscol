our mission

a marketplace built for human artists.
not generated content.

ai tools are flooding creative markets with generated work that undercuts real human skill and buries it. we built artizans collective to push back — a place where verified humans get clear visibility, fair pricing, and clients who actually want the real thing.

the problem

ai is making it harder to find and hire real artists.

flooded search results

generated images, ai-written copy and fake portfolios are crowding out real creators in every category. it's getting hard to tell what's real.

race to the bottom on price

when ai can pump out endless content for next to nothing, real artists get pushed to undercut their own rates just to keep up. that's not fair on anyone.

no verification anywhere

most platforms have no way to confirm a portfolio is real human work. you don't actually know what you've hired until the project's already going.

how it works

verified profiles. direct booking. no guesswork.

three things that shape everything we build.

01

human verification

every profile gets checked by our team before it goes live. we make sure the work is genuinely human-made, not generated stuff passed off as real creative practice.

02

clear pricing

artists put a starting price on their profile, so you know roughly what to expect before you reach out. no awkward back-and-forth just to work out basic scope.

03

direct booking

you send a brief straight from the artist's profile. no auctions, no middlemen — just a clear request the artist can read and accept.

what we stand for

a few things we won't budge on.

humans only

every profile here is a real person doing real creative work. no ai-generated portfolios, no fake artists, no content farms. that's not a rule we added — it's the whole point.

upfront by default

pricing, availability and how booking works are all visible from the start. we don't hide things behind sign-up walls. you should be able to decide before you reach out.

artists stay in control

artists choose when to publish, what to show and what to charge. profiles can go live or come down whenever. no lock-in, and no cut taken from direct bookings.
